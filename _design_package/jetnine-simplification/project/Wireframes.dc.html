What I assumed from the code and your answers
  • Clients are first-time or occasional flyers; staff are office people, not dispatchers.
  • Avinode does sourcing, operators, aircraft, airports and live ops → this admin becomes a client desk, and those five sections leave.
  • Keep the ink background, wordmark, Fraunces serif, one accent. Wireframes are drawn light only so they read on paper.
  • Desktop first. Mobile follows the same structure.
Rules every wireframe follows
  • Plain words: passengers, flight, city names, “reply due”. Dictionary in 1j.
  • One job per screen, one obvious next button.
  • Numbers only when they help a decision. No stat bars.
  • Fewer places: nav 7 → 4, account 7 tiles → 3 tabs, admin 15 → 5.
