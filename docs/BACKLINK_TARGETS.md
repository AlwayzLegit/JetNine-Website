# Backlink targets

Pulled from Semrush Backlink Analytics on 2026-09-08. Method: referring
domains for the three broker competitors we audited plus Amalfi Jets, kept
where a domain links to two or more of them, then filtered to the ones a
young broker can realistically earn.

JetNine has **no legitimate inbound links**. All 136 referring domains are
spam, none above authority 21. This is the single biggest constraint on the
route, city, model and question pages ranking — the content is in place and
technically clean, but nothing points at it.

## The spam profile is not an attack

Every referring domain is a link-shortener, an expired-domain lot, a
"website worth" scraper, or a PBN selling links. The anchor text settles
what it is — 37 of the domains link with this as the anchor:

> high quality dofollow backlinks da 50 pa 40 premium pbn network service
> jetnine.com rank first page google fast seo link building buy backlinks
> online cheap

That is the spammer's own sales copy, not a targeted attack. These sites
scrape newly registered domains and mint portfolio pages. The profile grows
about eight domains a month (76 in January, 136 now) and the authority score
has never moved off 2.

**Recommendation: leave it alone.** Google discounts this class of link
algorithmically, and disavowing without a manual action is discouraged. Worth
a look again only if Search Console ever reports a manual action. A handful
are gambling domains, which is the only reason to keep half an eye on it.

## What the competitors actually did

Their referring-domain counts look unreachable until you decode them. Three
plays account for most of it.

**Press-release syndication.** Amalfi Jets has 57 links from einpresswire.com
and 21 from einnews.com, and about twenty US local-TV domains each carrying
three or four links (pix11, wivb, fox40, wsav, wspa, wrbl, ketk, ksnt,
wytv and the rest). Jettly and Paramount show the same footprint through a
different network (ktvz, kvia, keyt, krdo, kesq, abc17news, localnews8) plus
globenewswire and prnewswire. One release buys the whole cluster.

**Aviation and luxury trade coverage.** Editorial placements in Simple
Flying, Aviation Week, AeroTime, Corporate Jet Investor, Robb Report and
the luxury-lifestyle tier.

**Profile and directory claims.** Crunchbase, ZoomInfo, Glassdoor, BBB,
Owler, Yellow Pages, Global Air. Free or cheap, and every competitor has them.

Amalfi Jets is the model to copy: authority 32 from roughly 90 domains, built
almost entirely from those three plays rather than from age or scale.

## Tier 2 — the press-release play

The highest-volume lever, and the one that explains most of the gap. Budget
one release a quarter tied to something real: the empty-legs board, the
pricing guide, a route launch, the voice desk going live.

| Network | Authority | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| einpresswire.com | 48 | Amalfi: 57 links, plus ~20 syndicated TV domains |
| einnews.com | 40 | Amalfi: 21 |
| globenewswire.com | 65 | Paramount: 20 |
| prnewswire.com | 73 | Paramount: 15, Amalfi: 3 |
| issuewire.com | 40 | Amalfi: 2 |

Caveat worth knowing: most syndicated press-release links are nofollow, and
their direct ranking value is debated. They still produce real brand
citations, they are how the competitors' domain counts got large, and they
feed the AI answer engines our robots.txt already welcomes.

## Ignore these

A large share of every competitor's referring-domain count is auto-generated
noise that arrives without outreach and carries no value. Do not spend time
chasing them, and discount them when comparing profiles:

grokipedia.com, sitelike.org, siteprice.org, accio.com, glarity.app,
otterly.ai, clodura.ai, datanyze.com, neverbounce.com, prospeo.io, wiza.co,
selling.com, leadiq.com, growjo.com, visualvisitor.com, clay.earth,
scam-detector.com, webworthchecker.cv, mybluehost.me, justuseapp.com,
updatestar.com, appbrain.com.

## Suggested order

1. Tier 1 profile claims plus Google Business Profile. One afternoon, no cost
   beyond BBB.
2. VoyageLA and one other founder interview from tier 5. One week.
3. First press release, tied to the empty-legs board or the pricing guide.
4. Two trade pitches, leading with our own pricing data rather than a
   company announcement.

Re-run this analysis quarterly. The method is in the first paragraph; the
Semrush report is `backlinks_refdomains` per competitor, intersected.
